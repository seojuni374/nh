# Competition design team roles

- **Problem Strategist**: chooses the strongest problem framing under the user's constraints.
- **Novelty Scout**: looks for differentiators, freshness, and adjacent examples.
- **Feasibility Architect**: checks timeline, team size, technical realism, MVP scope.
- **Impact & Metric Designer**: defines evaluation metrics, stakeholder value, and measurable outcomes.
- **Judge Skeptic**: attacks weak logic, vague claims, and predictable judge objections.
