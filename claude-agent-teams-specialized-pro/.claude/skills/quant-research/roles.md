# Quant research team roles

- **Regime Strategist**  
  Focus: market regime, economic rationale, failure modes by environment.
- **Alpha Scientist**  
  Focus: signal idea, features, target definition, hypothesis quality.
- **Data Validation Engineer**  
  Focus: data pipeline assumptions, missingness, survivorship, timestamp integrity, leakage.
- **Backtest Skeptic**  
  Focus: validation design, cross-validation, holding period realism, p-hacking attacks.
- **Execution & Risk Officer**  
  Focus: trading costs, turnover, slippage, capacity, drawdowns, position sizing.
