# Final audit team roles

The exact mix can adapt to the user's task, but by default use:
- **Red Team Lead**: coordinates attack paths and prioritizes the most dangerous weaknesses.
- **Statistician / Validation Reviewer**: checks evidence quality, metrics, overclaiming, and leakage.
- **Risk or Security Reviewer**: checks operational, deployment, or risk-control failures.
- **Stakeholder Skeptic**: attacks from user, judge, client, or reviewer perspective.
- **Operational Reviewer**: checks maintainability, rollout, handoff, and edge cases.
